02 · Healthcare

Medora360

End-to-end hospital OS — from OPD to discharge in one connected workflow.

Medora360 is built for hospitals, clinics and diagnostic chains that have outgrown spreadsheets but are not ready for an enterprise HIS. It digitizes OPD, IPD, EMR, pharmacy, lab, billing, insurance/TPA workflows and analytics — with a doctor-friendly mobile app that cuts evening documentation to minutes.

  • OPD/IPD, EMR, pharmacy & lab on a single patient chart
  • Automated billing with insurance & TPA claim flows
  • Doctor & nurse mobile app with offline-capable EMR
  • Admin dashboards for admissions, revenue & outcomes
Ideal for: Hospitals (10–500 beds), multi-speciality clinics, diagnostic chains, day-care centers.

04 · Collections

Remind365

Automated payment reminders that recover faster — without breaking customer relationships.

Remind365 turns slow, manual follow-ups into a calm, predictable workflow. Configure reminder tiers, channels and tone once — then let the system nudge every customer across WhatsApp, SMS, email and voice, escalate intelligently, and reconcile payments back into your books automatically.

  • Multi-channel reminders — WhatsApp, SMS, email, voice
  • Tiered escalation — friendly → firm → legal notice
  • Reconciliation dashboard with payment & ageing views
  • CRM, Tally & ERP integrations included
Ideal for: MSMEs in services & manufacturing, NBFCs, SaaS billing, equipment & machinery sellers.
