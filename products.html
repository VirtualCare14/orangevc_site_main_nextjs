03 · Patient Engagement

Orange Pulse – AI-Powered Patient Engagement Platform

Overview

Orange Pulse is an intelligent patient engagement platform developed by Orange Virtual Global Solutions Pvt. Ltd. to help hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centers, and healthcare providers streamline patient communication, automate appointment management, and enhance the overall patient experience.

Built with AI at its core, Orange Pulse acts as a virtual healthcare assistant that manages patient interactions across voice calls, WhatsApp, websites, and other digital channels—reducing administrative workload while improving patient satisfaction and operational efficiency.

Whether you're a single-doctor clinic or a multi-specialty hospital, Orange Pulse enables faster patient access, minimizes missed appointments, and ensures every patient receives timely, personalized communication.

Key Features

  • AI-powered appointment booking through Voice, WhatsApp, and Website
  • Automated appointment reminders and follow-ups
  • 24×7 virtual patient assistance
  • Intelligent call handling with multilingual support
  • Patient query management
  • Real-time appointment dashboard and analytics
  • Integration with Hospital Management Systems (HMS) and EHR platforms
  • Doctor availability and schedule management
  • Missed call and lead capture automation
  • Secure cloud-based infrastructure
Ideal for: Hospitals, Clinics, Diagnostic Centers, Single-Doctor Clinics & Healthcare Providers.
